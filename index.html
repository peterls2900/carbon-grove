Logbook

Gold Standard terms used in the game
PERs (Planned Emission Reductions)

Ex-ante units. After Design Certification, or after any Performance Certification, an A/R project may issue PERs for up to 5 years of modelled sequestration, but only for areas where a VVB has confirmed the trees are planted. Optional. PERs can be transferred and assigned but cannot be retired.

GS VERs

Ex-post units. At a Performance Certification the VVB verifies the actual carbon stock. Verified sequestration first converts outstanding PERs into GS VERs, replacing them in whoever's account holds them. Anything above that is issued as new GS VERs.

Compliance Buffer

20% of all PERs and GS VERs issued go into the Gold Standard Compliance Buffer. If the project falls into a shortfall, an equal number of buffer units is put on hold until it recovers.

Carbon performance

Units issued (GS VERs plus unconverted PERs) must not exceed the project's carbon stock. If they do, the project is in a performance shortfall: no new issuance until fixed by cancelling its own unsold units, buying GS VERs from other Gold Standard projects, or replanting. Not resolved within 5 years means a non-conformity.

Retirement

The final owner permanently retires GS VERs in the Gold Standard Impact Registry, in a named beneficiary's name. Retired serials can never be sold or claimed again.

Based on Gold Standard for the Global Goals GHG Emissions Reduction & Sequestration Product Requirements (Land Use & Forests rules, Annex C). Simplified: prices, growth and risk are invented, and some details have changed in newer versions of the requirements. Check the current documents before relying on any number.
